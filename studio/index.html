Sustainability Teaching Studio

Course plans of 7 or 14 sessions for a sustainability course built on INSEAD cases. Each plan can be taught in person or digitally. Most cases exist as a paper case and as a live, AI or VR version.

The course is research-driven. Its cases, simulations and frameworks come from a research programme on sustainable operations, circularity and environmental regulation, presented in the academic keynote (opens in new tab).

Overview

Structure

The course is built from ten modules in four parts. Circularity follows decarbonization because renewable energy raises demand for materials (solar panels, wind turbines, batteries) and creates new end-of-life waste.

PartModuleMain casesSessions in 14-session plan

7-session plan: choose 7 of the 10 modules, one session each. Commons and Economics of circularity are required. 14-session plan: all ten modules; four of them run over two sessions.

Each module, and each session within it, also works on its own and can be embedded in an existing course, for example in strategy, operations, finance or leadership.
