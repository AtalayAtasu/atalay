The physical disruption keeps growing. The rules swing back and forth.

The disruption
$40Binsured losses from the Los Angeles fires, January 2025: the costliest wildfire on record
27,600companies hit by the Valencia floods, October 2024; car plants 300 km away stopped for lack of parts
$107Binsured catastrophe losses in 2025, the sixth year in a row above $100B
The rules
2024–25The six largest US banks leave the Net-Zero Banking Alliance.
Aug 2025The alliance suspends its activities.
Mar 2026EU Omnibus in force: sustainability reporting narrowed to the largest firms; climate transition plans dropped from due diligence.

The rules may change. The disruption won’t go away. Treat it as a business disruption, not as ideology or compliance.

Sources: Swiss Re Institute, sigma 1/2026; Fundació Horta Sud (Dec 2024); Everstream Analytics; Directive (EU) 2026/470; press coverage of NZBA exits (2024–25).
